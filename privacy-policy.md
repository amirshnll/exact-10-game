# Privacy Policy for Exact 10

Exact 10 does not collect personal information, browsing history, website content, location data, or advertising identifiers. The game runs entirely within the extension popup and does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services. No data is sent to a server, sold, or shared with third parties.

## Data Stored

Game preferences such as language, numeral style, sound, and difficulty are stored locally, along with game statistics such as score, streak, number of attempts, and response time. This data is removed when the extension data is cleared or the extension is uninstalled.

## Why the `storage` Permission Is Required

The `storage` permission is used only to preserve game preferences and statistics between sessions. It does not provide access to personal files, browsing history, or website content, and it is not used to transmit data over the internet.

## External Link

The donation link opens an external website only when selected by the user. The privacy policy of that website applies after the user leaves the extension.
